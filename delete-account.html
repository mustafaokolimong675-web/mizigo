Delete your account

You can delete your Mizigo account and its data at any time. This page explains how, what gets deleted, and what has to be kept.

The fastest way: in the app

  • Open Mizigo and sign in.
  • Go to Settings → Password and security.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion starts immediately and you are signed out. There is no waiting period and no need to contact us.

If you cannot sign in

Send us a request from the email address on your Mizigo account, and we will verify it and delete the account for you.

Email a deletion request

This opens your email app with the request already written. Send it from the address on the account so we can verify it is you. We confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in credentials and profile — name, email address, phone number and role
  • If you are an account owner: your properties, units, tenancies, tenant records, staff assignments, maintenance reports and nominated payout details

What may be kept, and why

Certain financial and transaction records may be retained where necessary for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or another legal obligation — in Uganda this is generally up to five years. Where retention applies we keep only what is necessary for that purpose, and delete or anonymise it once that period ends.

  • Payment records — typically the amount, date and reference number, because money that changed hands has to remain explainable.
  • A landlord’s own records — if you are a tenant, the record that rent was paid on a unit stays with your landlord’s account as their business record.
  • Google Play purchase history — held by Google under Google’s policy, not ours.
Cancelling a subscription is not the same thing

Cancelling in Google Play stops the billing but leaves your account and its records in place. To remove the data, delete the account using one of the routes above.
