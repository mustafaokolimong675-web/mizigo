Privacy Policy

Mizigo is a property management app for landlords, their staff and their tenants. It records properties, tenancies and rent payments. This policy explains what it collects, uses and protects.

Last updated: 11 September 2026 App ID: ug.moonsoftware.mizigo

Mizigo is operated by Moonsoftware, Uganda. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Moonsoftware, and “the app” means the Mizigo app.

What we collect

Your account

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Your role on the account: owner, manager, caretaker, accountant or tenant

Collected when you create an account, or when a landlord adds you to theirs. Used to sign you in and to decide what you are allowed to see.

Property and tenancy records

  • Properties, addresses, units and rent amounts
  • Tenant names, phone numbers, email addresses, units and deposits
  • Maintenance reports

Entered by landlords and their staff. Used to run the account.

Payment records

  • Amount, date, method, transaction or reference number, bank or mobile money provider
  • Who recorded a payment and who approved it
  • Receipt numbers issued on approval

These are records about rent that has already been paid elsewhere. Mizigo does not move money and does not process rent payments. It stores the proof so the rent record, the receipt and the account remain explainable.

Where rent should arrive

If you are an account owner, the mobile money numbers and bank account number you nominate as your payout details. These are shown to your tenants so they know where to send rent, and are used to support the account.

Photographs of receipts

Receipt images never leave your device

When you scan a bank slip or choose a mobile money screenshot, the image is read on your phone using Google ML Kit’s on-device text recognition. Only the fields Mizigo extracts are stored and used.

The app contains no image-upload code, no cloud storage SDK and no crash-reporting or analytics SDK that could capture the image in the background.

Permissions and device access

Mizigo does not request access to your photo library. When you upload a mobile money screenshot, Android’s own picker opens and you choose one file; the app receives only that file.

The services we use

Mizigo uses the following services, which process data on our behalf as our processors:

  • Google Firebase Authentication — sign-in, including Google Sign-In. Handles your password, so we never store or see it.
  • Google Cloud Firestore — the database where your records are stored.
  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition — reads receipts on your device. The model runs locally; the image is not sent anywhere.
  • Google Play Billing — handles subscription payments.
What Mizigo does not include

The app ships with no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting and no push-notification SDK. There is no Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging or Cloud Storage SDK.

Subscription payments

For Mizigo subscriptions, payment credentials are processed by Google Play against the payment method on your Google account. Mizigo does not receive or store your card number or any payment credentials.

This is separate from the rent payment records described above, which are details you or your landlord type or scan in — amounts, dates, transaction reference numbers and nominated payout details.

When you share a receipt yourself

Mizigo can prepare a receipt, an invite or a report and hand it to another app you choose — your SMS app, WhatsApp, email or a file manager. That sharing is started by you and completed by the app you choose.

Other disclosure

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with any third party for their own purposes. We may disclose information where we are required to by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Who can see what

Mizigo restricts data by role, and this is enforced on the server by database security rules, not just in the app:

  • Owners see their whole portfolio — every property, every tenant, every payment on their account.
  • Managers and caretakers see only the properties they were assigned. They never see another property’s tenants, payments or reports.
  • Accountants read the portfolio and approve nothing.
  • Tenants see only their own unit, their own rent, their own receipts and notifications addressed to them. A tenant cannot see another tenant’s records.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Mizigo account and its data at any time, in either of two ways:

  • In the app — open Settings → Password and security → Delete account, confirm, and the deletion begins immediately.
  • On the web — use the account deletion request page, or email us from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my Mizigo account”.

What is deleted: your sign-in credentials and profile (name, email, phone, role), and — if you are an account owner — your properties, units, tenancies, tenant records, staff assignments, maintenance reports and nominated payout details.

What may be retained, and why: certain financial and transaction records may be retained where necessary for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or another legal obligation.

If you are a tenant: your landlord holds the tenancy record, and the rent they received is their own business record. Deleting your account removes your access and your personal record, but not the landlord’s account record.

Your other rights

  • See and correct your own details in the app, under Settings → Profile.
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing us. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play → Subscriptions. Cancelling a subscription does not delete your account.
  • Object or complain — write to us at the address below, and we will deal with it.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored in Google Cloud Firestore, protected by access rules that enforce the role restrictions described above. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is separated from the data layer.

Children

Mizigo is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child, we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially we will say so in the app before the change takes effect, and we will update the date at the top of this page.
